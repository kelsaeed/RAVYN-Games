PRIVACY

A raven will take anything shiny that is not nailed down. Your data is nailed down. Here is the honest, slightly unhinged version of what that means.

1

What we collect right now

Nothing. Not a byte.

This site is a prototype. There is no analytics script, no tag manager, no pixel, no cookie banner — because there is no cookie to warn you about. The page loads, it shows you a metal bird, it forgets you completely.

2

What your browser does anyway

Two things happen that we do not control and should still tell you about:

  • Fetching three.js, GSAP and Lenis from cdnjs and jsDelivr. Those CDNs see the request, like every CDN does.
  • Whoever ends up hosting this will keep ordinary server logs — IP, timestamp, which file.

That is the entire surface. It is smaller than most.

3

If you email us

Then we have your email, obviously. We keep it in a normal inbox, we reply to it, and we do not add you to a list you did not ask for. There is no list. We have not built the list. Building the list sounds exhausting.

We are not going to
sell anything about you.
There is nothing to sell.

4

Things we will never do

  • Sell your data to advertisers
  • Fingerprint your browser
  • Follow you around the internet
  • Hide a tracker inside a cursor
  • Pretend the list above is a legal guarantee written by a lawyer. It is not. See the flag at the top.
5

Your rights

Under GDPR and friends you can ask what we hold, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. For this site the honest answer to the first question is "an email, if you sent us one", and the answer to the last is "done".

Ask at hello@ravyngames.com.

6

When this becomes a real game studio site

It will grow: a launcher, a newsletter, crash reports, maybe a store. Every one of those collects something real, and every one of them needs a clause here written by someone qualified.

When that day comes this page gets rewritten properly. It can stay this loud. It cannot stay this vague.

7

Changes

If we change this, the date below changes with it. We will not quietly rewrite history and hope you were not looking. Ravens notice that sort of thing.
